            Data & Privacy

            Your scraper configurations, snapshots, monitoring history, preferences, and free-trial run count are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage) — never uploaded anywhere.

            Scraped page content stays on your device. It only ever leaves the browser when YOU explicitly export it (CSV/Excel/JSON) or download images/files — both save directly to your own computer via Chrome's Downloads feature. Fetching images for a ZIP bundle downloads them straight from the site that hosts them, not through any Web Scraper server. This extension currently has no connected backend, no account system, and sends no analytics or telemetry of any kind.

            If you purchase or recover a license in a future version, only your email and license key are ever sent to Web Scraper's license server to verify your purchase — never any scraped page content or browsing data.
